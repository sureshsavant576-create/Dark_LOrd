# Repository & Project Guidelines

## Git & Repository Rules
- **Target Repository**: Always push commits and changes to `https://github.com/sureshsavant576-create/Dark_LOrd` (`origin`).
- **Single Branch Only**: Always work on and push to **one branch only** (`main`). Do not create or switch to separate feature branches.
- Remote `origin` must remain configured to `https://github.com/sureshsavant576-create/Dark_LOrd.git`.

## Prototype Development Constraints
- **Avoid Login**: Do not include login pages, sign-in forms, or authentication barriers in prototypes. All features must be directly accessible immediately without requiring authentication.
- **Size Limit (< 10 MB)**: Keep the entire repository, dependencies, and asset footprint strictly under 10 MB. Avoid heavy media, unnecessary packages, or large binaries.
